### Vocab — People
| Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|

| مَرَة | mara | woman |
| زَلَمة | zalame* | man |
| رِجّال | ríjjaal | man |

*"zalame" is one of the rare masculine nouns with a ta'marbouta.

### Grammar — Sun & Moon Letters + definite article "il-"
- Definite article: **íl-** = "the"
- Moon letters: ا ب ت ث ج ح خ د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ع غ ف ق ك ل م ن ه و ي (full alphabet shown as moon-letter chart; sun letters below take a different subset)
- Sun letters group shown: ا ب ت ث ج ح خ / د ذ ر ز س ش ص / ض ط ظ ع غ ف ق / ك ل م ن ه و ي — (source presents both moon and sun sets across this same letter grid; sun letters assimilate the "l" of "il-")
- Example moon letters (íl- stays as íl-, "l" pronounced):
  - íl-mara — المَرة — the woman
  - íl-walad — الوَلَد — the boy
  - íl-2aSiir — القَصِير — the short (m)
  - íl-bínt — البِنْت — the girl
- Example sun letters ("l" assimilates into first consonant, doubling it):
  - íz-zalame — الَّزَلمة — the man
  - ír-ríjjaal — الِّرّجال — the men/man
  - íT-Tawiil — الطَّوِيل — the tall (m)

### Example Phrases — Person & Adjective (three sentence patterns: "A", "The", "Is")
Pattern group 1 (walad/bínt + 2aSiir/2aSiire):

|---|---|---|---|
| A | وَلَد قَصِير | walad 2aSiir | a short boy |
| The | الوَلَد القَصِير | íl-walad íl-2aSiir | the short boy |
| Is | الوَلَد قَصِير | íl-walad 2aSiir | the boy is short |
| A | بِنْت قَصِيرة | bínt 2aSiire | a short girl |
| The | البِنْت القَصِيرة | íl-bínt íl-2aSiire | the short girl |
| Is | البِنْت قَصِيرة | íl-bínt 2aSiire | the girl is short |

Pattern group 2 (zalame/mara + Tawiil/Tawiile):
| Type | Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | زَلَمة طَوِيل | zalame Tawiil | a tall man |
| The | الَّزَلمة الطَّوِيل | íz-zalame íT-Tawiil | the tall man |
| Is | الَّزَلمة طَوِيل | íz-zalame Tawiil | the man is tall |
| A | مَرَة طَوِيلة | mara Tawiile | a tall woman |

| Is | المَرة طَوِيلة | íl-mara Tawiile | the woman is tall |

*Note from source: these are the three primary ways to combine a noun and adjective in Arabic, including "no-verb" sentences.*

## Conversation Group

### Greeting Basics
| Function | Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| My name is | أنا إسْمي... | ana ísmi... | my name is [name] |
| I'm | أنا... | ana... | I'm [name] |
| Hello | مَرْحَبا | mar7aba | hello |
| Welcome/hi | أهْلاً | ahlan | welcome |
| Hi (response) | أهْلين | ahleen | hi (response) |

Note: إسم (ísím) = "name"; suffix ـي = "my" → إسمي (ísmi) = "my name" (the verb "is" is implied).

Combined together:
| Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|
| مَرْحَبا أنا... | mar7aba, ana... | hello, I'm... |
| أهلاً أنا إسْمي... | ahlan, ana ísmi... | welcome, my name is... |
| مَرْحَبا أنا إسْمي... | mar7aba, ana ísmi... | hello, my name is... |

Follow-up (gendered "what's your name?"):
| Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|
| وإنْتَ شو إسْمَك؟ | w-ínte, shu ísmak? | and you (m), what's your name? |
| وإنْتي شو إسِمِك؟ | w-ínti, shu ísmek? | and you (f), what's your name? |

### Greetings: Time of Day

| Opening | صَباح الخير | Sabaa7 íl-kheer | Good Morning! | "morning of goodness" |

## Extraction notes / decisions
- Both "ríjjaal" (man/men, could be sg. or collective per source) and "zalame" mean "man" — kept both, distinct vocab entries, as source presents them separately (ríjjaal marked with an "N" pin icon, zalame marked with "S" pin icon in the vocab card — i.e., **ríjjaal = Northern, zalame = Southern** for "man"). Per project's Southern-primary rule, "zalame" should be treated as primary/canonical, "ríjjaal" as accepted Northern alternate.
- Similarly Colors & Clothes vocab: "bluuze" marked (S), "kanze" marked (N) for "t-shirt" — bluuze primary, kanze accepted alternate.
- Reading Exercise sentences have no per-item English gloss in the source; English glosses above are inferred from vocab tables, not present verbatim in source — flag for app-build to confirm/regenerate translations at data-entry time rather than treat as directly-sourced English.
