### Example Sentence Cheatsheet — Lesson 1

**Happy or Sad:**
1. ínte mabSuut? / ee, ana mabsuuT. (N) — "Are you (m) happy? Yes, I am happy."
2. ínte za3laan? / aa, ana za3laan. (S) — "Are you (m) sad? Yes, I am sad."
3. ínti mabsuuTa? / laa, ana za3laane. — "Are you (f) happy? No, I am sad."
4. ínti za3laane? / laa, ana mabsuuTa. — "Are you (f) sad? No, I am happy."

**Describing People:**
1. íl-mara mabsuuTa. hiyye laabse kanze khaDra. (N) — "The woman is happy, she is wearing a green t-shirt."
2. ísh-shabb za3laan. huwwa laabís bluuze zar2a. (S) — "The guy is sad. he is wearing a blue t-shirt."
3. íl-bínt za3laane? la, híyye mnii7a. (N) — "The girl is sad? No, she is good."
4. ísh-shabb ta3baan. laabís kanze khaDra. (N) — "The tired guy is wearing a blue t-shirt." *(note: source English translation says "blue" but Arabic/romanization says khaDra = green — likely a source typo; flagging, not correcting the source data)*
5. íl-walad íl-mabsuuT laabes kanze Safra. (N) — "The happy boy is wearing a yellow t-shirt."

### Example Sentence Cheatsheet — Lesson 2 (Describing People)
1. fii zalame Tawiil. huuwa laabes bluuze khaDra u banTaloon a7mar. (S) — "There is a tall man. He is wearing a green t-shirt and a red pants."
2. fii walad 2aSiir. huwwe waa2ef u laabes kanze. (N) — "There is a short boy. He is standing and wearing a t-shirt."

5. ana shaayfe íl-bínt. híyye laabse fustaan azra2 7ílu. (S) — "I (f) see the girl. she is wearing a blue nice dress."

## Conversation — Exchanging Pleasantries
*Source note: "These pleasantries build on the greetings you learned last week."*

### How Are You?
| Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|
| كيفَك / كيفِك | kiifak (m) / kiifek (f) | how are you? |

### Response 1
| Step | Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Answer | أنا مِنيح / مِنيَحَة | ana mnii7 (m) / mnii7a (f) | I'm good |
| 2. Praise (optional) | الحَمدِلله | íl-7amdílla | praise be to god |
| 3. Return | وإنت كيفَك؟ / وإنتي كيفِك؟ | u-ínta kiifak (m)? / u-ínti kiifek (f)? | and how are you? |

Note: **In Southern**, "Good" is كَوَّيس (kwayyes, M) or كَوَيسة (kwayse, F) — i.e., kwayyes/kwayse is the Southern-dialect alternative to mnii7/mnii7a for "good."

### Response 2 (Simplified)
| Step | Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Answer | كِل شي مِنيح | kíl shi mnii7 | everything's good |
| 2. Praise (optional) | الحَمدِلله | íl-7amdílla | praise be to god |
| 3. Return | وإنت؟ / وإنتي؟ | u-ínta (m)? / u-ínti (f)? | and you? |

Note: كِل (kíl) = "all," شي (shi) = "thing" → كِل شي (kíl shi) = "everything."

### Example Exchanges
| Arabic | Romanization | English |
|---|---|---|
| كيفِك؟ – أنا مْنيحَة الحَمدِلله، وإنتي كيفِك؟ | kiifek? — ana mnii7a íl-7amdílla, u-ínti kiifek? | How are you (f)? — I'm good, praise be to god, and how are you (f)? |
